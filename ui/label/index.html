How to label
  • Observed: add one interval per continuous visit or bout. Use the playhead buttons or type seconds.
  • Not observed: an explicit negative after reviewing the whole clip.
  • Cannot tell: ambiguous or unavailable evidence; excluded from accuracy. Barking needs usable audio.
  • Safety Watch concerns visible dangerous circumstances. Mask interaction alone does not establish ingestion or entanglement.
